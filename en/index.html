Kit72: Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026 · Android app “Kit72” (gr.kit72.app) · Impulse Tinkerings

In short

The app does not collect, send or sell any personal data. Everything you enter is stored only on your phone. There are no accounts, ads, usage analytics or tracking.

What is stored on your phone

  • The people in your household: name, month and year of birth, and any needs you enter (e.g. medicine, glasses, pregnancy, mobility).
  • Pets, car, diet and area, as you enter them.
  • Your kit list: items, quantities, expiry dates, where each item is kept.
  • Your emergency plan: meeting points, phone numbers and notes.
  • App settings (language, appearance, reminders).

This information is used only to build and update your list inside the app. It never leaves your phone unless you act.

When you choose to share something

  • Print / PDF / message: the app creates the document on your phone and hands it to the app you pick (printer, email, messaging, etc.). The plan and wallet cards contain no health needs; the list shows items, so it may reveal some (e.g. one person’s medicines).
  • Backup: a file with all your data, including health needs, is created. It goes only where you send it; keep it somewhere safe.
  • Links: links to official sources (e.g. Civil Protection, national weather service, Meteoalarm) open in your browser; those websites’ own policies apply there.

Phone permissions

  • Notifications: for reminders about expiry dates, the seasonal check and your plan. They are scheduled locally on the phone and pass through no server.
  • Run at startup: so reminders are rescheduled after the phone restarts.

The app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Deletion

All data can be deleted in Settings → “Delete everything from this phone”, or by uninstalling the app. Since we keep no copies, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

The app is meant for adults preparing their household’s kit. Children’s details (name, month and year of birth) are entered by the parent and stay only on the parent’s phone.

Who is responsible

“Kit72” is an independent app by Impulse Tinkerings. It is not a government service and does not replace the instructions of the authorities. Contact: impulse.tinkerings@gmail.com

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the date at the top will be updated. If a future feature (e.g. a shared family list) ever needs to send data, it will be optional, described here before release, and will ask for your consent.
